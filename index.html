About GenerEdge

Where technology meets advocacy

Minority- and women-owned businesses are historically approved for loans at a rate roughly 50% lower than non-minority firms. We believe Equal Access Financing isn't just a tagline — it's an economic necessity.

Our AI-assisted loan origination platform serves a growing network of lenders who are actively seeking to deploy capital to women, minority, veteran and rural entrepreneurs across all 50 states.

  • The Backbone: partnership with Finturf, a Lending-as-a-Service platform
  • The Intelligence: SafeTurf.ai 360° — AI-driven KYB/KYC and fraud monitoring
  • The Standard: SOC 2 Type II audited for bank-level data security
